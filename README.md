# Project Zero

*They were never supposed to exist.*

Year 2096. Eight test subjects wake up inside NOVAGEN's underground facility with no memories, strange abilities and numbers instead of names. Only one of them will leave.

A retro pixel platformer and fighting game that runs in the browser, on desktop or phone.

## Modes
- **Story** – run, jump and fight through Sector 1 of the facility and recover your subject's name.
- **Arcade** – beat the other seven subjects in a row, then face Subject 00.
- **Versus** – any subject, any of 10 arenas, against the CPU or a friend (2P touch or 2P keyboard).

## Play locally
Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
